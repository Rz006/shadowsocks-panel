# Conversion Copywriting Guide

## Voice
- Write to one person ("you"). Short sentences. Concrete nouns and numbers.
- Reading level grade 6–8. Cut filler words: "innovative", "solutions", "cutting-edge", "world-class", "elevate", "seamless", "unlock".
- Match the brand: a law firm is calm and precise; a gym can be punchy; a café warm and sensory.

## Headline formulas (hero)
Pick one, then make it specific to the business:
1. **Outcome + timeframe/qualifier**: "Pain-free implants, done in a single visit."
2. **Outcome + for whom**: "Bookkeeping for Austin restaurants that hate paperwork."
3. **Problem → promise**: "Leaky pipe at 2am? A licensed plumber at your door in 60 minutes."
4. **Category + differentiator**: "The only CrossFit gym in Leeds with a free crèche."
5. **Sensory (hospitality)**: "Wood-fired Neapolitan pizza, 90 seconds from oven to table."

Subhead (1–2 lines): who it's for + how + proof point. "Family & cosmetic dentistry in South Austin. Same-week appointments, most insurance accepted, 4.9★ from 600+ Google reviews." (only real numbers)

## CTA wording
- First person + outcome: "Book my free consult", "Get my quote in 2 minutes", "Reserve a table", "Start my free trial".
- Reduce friction near the button: "No obligation · Takes 60 seconds · We reply within 1 hour".
- Avoid: "Submit", "Click here", "Learn more" as primary CTA.

## Section copy patterns
- **Problem/agitation**: 3 bullets in the customer's words ("You've put off the dentist because last time hurt").
- **Services**: name → benefit line → 1 detail → link. "Invisalign — Straighten your teeth without metal braces. Most cases in 6–12 months."
- **How it works**: 3 steps, verb-led ("Book online → Meet your dentist → Leave smiling").
- **Why us / differentiators**: specific, verifiable reasons (licenses, guarantees, response times, years).
- **Social proof**: quote a specific result with name + context. Ask the client for 3–6 real reviews; Google reviews can be quoted with permission.
- **Offer / pricing**: clear starting prices or packages if the client allows; anchor with the most popular option.
- **FAQ**: answer the top objections: price, time, pain/risk, process, guarantees, location/parking, insurance/payment.
- **Final CTA band**: restate the outcome + button + reassurance.

## PAS / AIDA for landing pages
- **PAS**: Problem → Agitate → Solution.
- **AIDA**: Attention (hero) → Interest (benefits) → Desire (proof, offer) → Action (CTA).

## Microcopy
- Form labels plain ("Your phone number"), helper text where needed ("We'll only call about your booking").
- Errors helpful ("Please enter a phone number with area code"), not "Invalid input".
- Success state sets expectations ("Thanks, Sara! We'll call you within 1 business hour.").

## SEO-aware copy
- Put the primary keyword naturally in H1, first paragraph, one H2, title tag and meta description.
- Use location naturally for local businesses ("dentist in South Austin").
- Write for humans first; never keyword-stuff.

## Honesty rules
- No invented testimonials, ratings, customer counts, awards, certifications, "as seen on" logos, or fake urgency (fake countdown timers, "only 2 left" when untrue).
- Claims of "best"/"#1" need a source; prefer specific facts.
- Health, legal and financial claims: keep factual and avoid guarantees of outcomes.
